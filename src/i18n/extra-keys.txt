Who can use Setu?
Final-year students at our partner college, and verified businesses in the pilot town.
Does it cost anything?
No. The pilot is free for students and businesses.
How do I know an opening is real?
Our team visits every business in person before it can post. Only openings from visited businesses are shown, with the date of the visit.
Will my internship count for credit?
Yes. When you finish, our team sends your college a completion letter that it can use for your AICTE credit.
Will I be paid?
Each opening shows the stipend up front, or says "Unpaid". You know before you apply.
I run a small business. How much time does this take?
Posting an opening takes about 10 minutes. After that, you only look at the students who apply.
What if an intern does not work out?
Tell our team. We will help you find a replacement from the applicant list.
